AI Solutions for DFT Engineers

  • AI is revolutionizing the way DFT engineers approach test challenges. Synopsys AI-driven TestMAX solutions bring a new level of intelligence and automation to the DFT process, empowering engineers to achieve higher quality results, faster and more efficiently than ever before.   • By integrating advanced AI across the TestMAX family, DFT engineers can confidently keep pace with growing design complexity, achieve ambitious quality and schedule goals, and deliver comprehensive, cost-effective test solutions for today’s most advanced SoCs.
