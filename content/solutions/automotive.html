Drive Success for Software-Defined Vehicles

style
pad-top-md, textcomp, purple, center

Synopsys accelerates innovation at automotive OEMs and Tier 1s with silicon to systems design solutions. Synopsys’ automotive solution enables hardware/software co-design to meet E/E, ECU, and SoC architecture requirements and expedites software development to reduce time to market while improving software quality with test automation. In addition, the solution ensures reliability by monitoring and analyzing critical silicon failures in-field throughout silicon lifecycle. Our comprehensive automotive-grade IP, virtual prototyping, design and verification, and silicon lifecycle management technologies help you create safe, secure, reliable, and high-quality software-defined vehicles.
