How can Synopsys help me define and optimize your modern SoC and E/E architecture?

Synopsys provides hardware/software co-design capabilities to optimize SoC and E/E architecture for better PPA, safety, reliability, quality, and security. HW/SW co-design enables design teams to more easily identify potential trade-offs and make the best decisions based on market demands and system requirements. The earlier that important architectural decisions can be made, the sooner OEMs and Tier 1s can start focusing on design and development.

​

Synopsys also provides wide range of products and services to enable HW/SW Co-design, such as consultation from automotive industry expert, Platform Architect, the AI-enabled EDA suite, multi-die solution, automotive-grade IP, AI Accelerators (Sima.ai ML Platform and IP), virtual prototyping and test automation solutions to help you develop electronic Digital Twin.

How does Synopsys support the development of ISO 26262 functional safety and ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity compliant systems?

Synopsys offers a comprehensive set of integrated, standards-based Silicon Lifecycle Management (SLM) tools, IP, and methodologies that provide observability, analytics, and automation at the silicon level. Our Process, Voltage, and Temperature (PVT) Monitor IP, for example, is certified as ASIL-B Ready and meets the AEC-Q100 Grade 2 standard. 

​

We also provide the broadest portfolio of automotive-grade IP. This includes interface, processor, security, and foundation IP that help accelerate SoC-level design and qualification. These automotive-grade IP solutions and the continuous insights from SLM are essential for ensuring the long-term functional safety of modern vehicles.

What is the role of Synopsys' electronics digital twins (eDT) in the automotive development process?

Synopsys' electronics digital twins (eDT) play a crucial role in the automotive development process by allowing you to develop and validate each step before production, from SoC to ECU to E/E Architecture. This ensures that potential issues are addressed early, optimizing the design for performance, safety, reliability, quality, and security.

What technologies does Synopsys offer to accelerate automotive system development?

Synopsys offers Silicon to System design solutions to accelerate and innovate automotive system development process with wide range of solutions ranging from auto grade Silicon IPs to eDT solutions that allows you to shift left the entire software development and validation cycle.

How does Synopsys ensure the success of automotive projects from early architecture to final delivery?

Synopsys ensures the success of automotive projects from early architecture to final delivery by providing guidance and consultation from automotive industry experts, architecture exploration tools for general SoCs to AI SoCs, and Embedded level system simulation and validation solutions.
