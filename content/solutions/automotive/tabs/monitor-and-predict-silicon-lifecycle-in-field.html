Monitor and Predict Silicon Lifecycle In-Field

The Synopsys SLM product family allows OEMs to continuously monitor critical metrics such as silicon voltage, temperature, margins, and health, enhancing the reliability and performance of ECUs, CPUs, GPUs, and architectures. Synopsys SLM products utilize real-time edge and cloud analytics to assess silicon aging and reliability, offering valuable insights into both systemic and random defects within your vehicles and across fleet. It ensures silicon reliability and provides predictive maintenance capabilities, extending silicon lifecycle in field and lowering cost.

How does Synopsys' Silicon Lifecycle Management (SLM) product family enhance the reliability and performance of silicon components?

The Synopsys SLM product family enhances the reliability and performance of silicon components by continuously monitoring critical metrics such as silicon voltage, temperature, margins, and health. This helps ensure that ECUs, CPUs, GPUs, and other architectures maintain optimal performance and reliability.

What metrics are monitored by Synopsys Silicon Lifecycle Management (SLM) products to ensure silicon reliability?

Synopsys SLM products monitor critical metrics such as silicon voltage, temperature, margins, and health. By tracking these metrics, the SLM products can provide insights into the silicon's reliability and performance.

How do Synopsys Silicon Lifecycle Management (SLM) products utilize real-time edge and cloud analytics?

Synopsys SLM products utilize real-time edge and cloud analytics to assess silicon aging and reliability. This allows for the detection of both systemic and random defects within vehicles and across fleets, providing valuable insights and enabling predictive maintenance.

What insights do Synopsys Silicon Lifecycle Management (SLM) products offer regarding silicon aging and reliability?

Synopsys SLM products offer insights into both systemic and random defects affecting silicon aging and reliability. By assessing these factors in real-time, the SLM products help identify potential issues and provide predictive maintenance capabilities.

How do Synopsys Silicon Lifecycle Management (SLM) products contribute to predictive maintenance and extending silicon lifecycle?

Synopsys SLM products contribute to predictive maintenance by continuously monitoring silicon metrics and utilizing real-time analytics to assess reliability. This proactive approach helps extend the silicon lifecycle in the field and lowers maintenance costs by addressing potential issues before they lead to failures.
