Verification CoStart Services

Our services specialize in enhanced productivity with reduced risk

Synopsys offers a licenced CoStart Verification Service for formal verification, low power verification, static verification, and verification IP to accelerate the implementation of verification methodology. A 10-day service ensures deep engagement and assistance.

  • Formal Verification: Synopsys works with customers to add formal verification into their verification methodology.
  • Low Power Verification: Synopsys assists customer with setting up a low power static checking flow and simulation environment.
  • Static Verification CoStart: Synopsys enables customer with setting up an RTL signoff methodology and flow.
  • Verification IP: Synopsys helps customer to understand protocol basics to help integrate the VIP with their testbenches.

Synopsys Formal Consulting Services offer various options to suit the unique needs of our customers. From formal methodology training to turn-key projects, customers can customize and define the projects. Synopsys verification consulting team

  • Helps accelerate customer needs at Block, Subsystem and SoC level verification.
  • Assists with deployment of optimized methodologies and flow enhancements leveraging best practices.
  • Customized engagements to enable to enable solution delivery for customer specific scope and tasks.
  • Augments customer teams with domain experts (Protocol, Formal, Low Power, Static and Debug).
