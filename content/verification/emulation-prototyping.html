Software-Defined Hardware-Assisted Verification is Critical

overview

Synopsys ZeBu® and HAPS® systems are advanced emulation and prototyping solutions for software-defined hardware-assisted verification (HAV). Software-defined HAV enables up to 2x performance boost for ZeBu Server 5 and scales capacity up to 2x with modular HAV for AI-era mega designs. ZeBu delivers high-performance emulation, allowing for thorough verification of complex SoC designs with extensive debug capabilities. HAPS provides high performance prototyping capabilities, enabling early software development, system validation, and hardware-software integration, which accelerates time-to-market. Together, these tools enhance productivity by enabling parallel hardware and software development, reducing risks associated with late-stage design changes, and improving overall design quality and reliability.
